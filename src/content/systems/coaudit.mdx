CoAudit is a domain-specific audit agent that lives where auditors already
work — **inside Excel and Word.** Rather than asking people to leave their
tools for a chat window, it embeds in the document and does the review in place.

Crucially, every judgment comes with a **reasoning trace.** In audit, an
answer you can't defend is worse than no answer at all. Exposing *why* the
agent reached a conclusion is what makes its output reviewable instead of
something you have to take on faith.

## What it does

- **Embedded in Excel and Word** — no context-switching out of the workpaper.
- **Domain-specific** — grounded in audit conventions, not generic Q&A.
- **Reasoning traces on every judgment** — each conclusion shows its work.

This is the standard I wrote about in
[*Albert Einstein Walks Into a Library*](/writing/albert-einstein-library):
audit-grade AI has to be **repeatable, accurate, and auditable in itself.**
CoAudit is what that standard looks like in the tools auditors use every day.
