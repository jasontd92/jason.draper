Creed is an autonomous QA engineering engine. It runs continuously — 24/7 —
and scales horizontally: when there's more to test, you add more agents rather
than more people.

The premise is simple and a little uncomfortable: **demand for QA is
effectively infinite.** There is always another edge case, another regression
surface, another flow that changed underneath you. Human QA is bounded by
hours in the day; an autonomous engine is bounded only by how well you
orchestrate it.

## What it does

- **Tests continuously**, not in batches tied to a release cadence.
- **Scales horizontally** — throughput grows with the number of agents you run.
- **Runs unattended**, so coverage doesn't stop when the team logs off.

Creed is one half of a pattern I keep coming back to: the hard part of
production AI systems isn't the model, it's the *orchestration* — keeping many
agents focused, grounded, and aggregating into something trustworthy. The same
lesson shows up in [audit-grade document review](/writing/albert-einstein-library).
